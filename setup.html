Setup Guide

Follow these steps to connect your Cortex Band to the internet and link it to your shopping cart.

1

Power On

Turn on your Cortex Band. If it hasn't been connected to your Wi-Fi before, it will automatically enter Setup Mode.

2

Connect to the Band

Open your phone's Wi-Fi settings and look for a new network called Cortex-Band-Setup. Tap to connect to it.

3

Enter Wi-Fi & Get Code

A popup window will automatically appear on your phone. It will display your unique 6-character Pairing Code at the top. Write it down, then select your home Wi-Fi network and enter the password so the band can connect!

Need to Reset?

If you need to connect to a different Wi-Fi network or unpair the device, simply hold the button on your band for 3 seconds. The band will vibrate/beep and restart the setup process.
